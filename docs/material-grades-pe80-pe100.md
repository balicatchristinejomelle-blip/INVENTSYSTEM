- **HDPE Standard, Commercial Grade (CGPE), and CTS Grade are not active Material Grades.** Their readable legacy definitions are intentionally retained.
- Applicable grade filters use **All Material Grades / PE80 / PE100**. Existing searchable Material Grade dropdowns remain searchable.

Grade validation restricts new selections without changing those calculations or identifiers.

- Localhost data was not wiped. No database/backend changes, dependencies, images, or LFS changes were made.
